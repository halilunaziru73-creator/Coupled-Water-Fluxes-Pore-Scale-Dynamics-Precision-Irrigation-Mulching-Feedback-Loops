Each of the following sections gathers everything about one result of the article under one heading: the problem and the author's contribution with its pinpointed equation, the worked numbers that can be checked on a calculator or in the workbook, graphs that mark the exact points, the figures of the article with their numbered labels, the derivations, and why it happens. The terms, the constants, the tables, the path from the data to every result, the data themselves and the origin of every item follow at the end. The derivations of this supplement are of established methods (spectral indices, FAO-56, the Richards equation, budgets, D8 flow and wetness, RUSLE, Latin-hypercube sampling), given so that each computation of the software can be followed. They are not claimed as new; the contribution of the article is the integration, the two rules of origin, and the verification that found defects (\cref{tab:novelty}).
