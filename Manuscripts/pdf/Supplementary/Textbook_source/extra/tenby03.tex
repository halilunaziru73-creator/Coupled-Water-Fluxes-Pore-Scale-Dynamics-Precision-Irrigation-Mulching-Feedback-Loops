\section{Worked solutions on ten crops (hot climate)}
The ten crops of the library differ in their demand, their roots and their sensitivity. The same type of question is answered for each of them at $ET_0=7.5$ mm\,d$^{-1}$; the data are typical values of the crops (Doorenbos--Kassam and FAO 56).

\exbox{Crop example 3c: Maize}{Maize on the loam S06: the trigger fires at the depletion fraction $p=0.55$. Using the demand $r=ET_c/TAW$ and $t_{\rm trig}=p/r$, find the time from full profile to the trigger and the number of irrigations in a season of 125 days.}\sol $TAW=1.7\times100=170$ mm; $r=1.20\times7.5/170=0.0529$ d$^{-1}$ \ans $t_{\rm trig}=0.55/0.0529=10.4$ d \ans Irrigations $\approx125/10.4=12$ \ans

\exbox{Crop example 3c: Wheat}{Wheat on the loam S06: the trigger fires at the depletion fraction $p=0.55$. Using the demand $r=ET_c/TAW$ and $t_{\rm trig}=p/r$, find the time from full profile to the trigger and the number of irrigations in a season of 150 days.}\sol $TAW=1.7\times120=204$ mm; $r=1.15\times7.5/204=0.0423$ d$^{-1}$ \ans $t_{\rm trig}=0.55/0.0423=13.0$ d \ans Irrigations $\approx150/13.0=12$ \ans
