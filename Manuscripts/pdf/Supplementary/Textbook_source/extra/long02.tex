\subsection{Complete calculation of flux in a layered profile}
\exbox{Darcy flux between three layers of a loam}{A loam profile has suctions of 100, 330 and 1000 cm at depths 30 cm apart. Find the conductivity of each layer from the van Genuchten model, the gradient and the flux (Darcy--Buckingham), and decide the direction of flow.}\sol
\ans Saturated conductivity of the loam $K_s=12.1$ mm\,h$^{-1}$ $=29.0$ cm\,d$^{-1}$
\ans Layer between suctions $100$ and $330$ cm, $\Delta z=30$ cm: geometric mean suction $182$ cm, $K_r=0.000201$, $K=0.00583$ cm\,d$^{-1}$; hydraulic gradient $(h_2-h_1)/\Delta z-1=6.67$; flux $q=-K\,dH/dz=0.0388$ cm\,d$^{-1}$ (upward)
\ans Layer between suctions $330$ and $1000$ cm, $\Delta z=30$ cm: geometric mean suction $574$ cm, $K_r=4.28e-06$, $K=0.000124$ cm\,d$^{-1}$; hydraulic gradient $(h_2-h_1)/\Delta z-1=21.33$; flux $q=-K\,dH/dz=0.00265$ cm\,d$^{-1}$ (upward)
\ans Layer between suctions $100$ and $200$ cm, $\Delta z=60$ cm: geometric mean suction $141$ cm, $K_r=0.000453$, $K=0.0132$ cm\,d$^{-1}$; hydraulic gradient $(h_2-h_1)/\Delta z-1=0.67$; flux $q=-K\,dH/dz=0.00877$ cm\,d$^{-1}$ (upward)
\ans The gradient exceeds 1 in the dry layers, so the water moves up toward the roots and surface, and is less than 1 where the profile drains; the sign of $(h_2-h_1)/\Delta z-1$ decides the direction
