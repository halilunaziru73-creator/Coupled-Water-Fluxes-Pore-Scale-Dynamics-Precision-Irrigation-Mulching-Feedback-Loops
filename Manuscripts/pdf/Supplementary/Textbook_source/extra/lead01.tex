\chapterlead{\subhd{After this chapter you can} describe soil as a three-phase system and compute its porosity, void ratio and degrees of saturation; classify a soil by texture; express soil water as content, depth and suction and convert between them; derive the water balance of a root zone; write the van Genuchten retention and Mualem conductivity functions and evaluate them; and reduce the field data of a profile to the numbers (total available water, allowed depletion, interval) that an irrigation schedule needs.}
