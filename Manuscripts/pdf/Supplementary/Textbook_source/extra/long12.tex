\subsection{Complete design: drip irrigation of a tomato field}
\exbox{Design of a drip system for 1 ha of tomatoes}{One hectare of tomatoes grows on the sandy loam S05. Laterals are 100 m long and 1.5 m apart, the inlet head is 10 m, the emitters are compared for three types, and the root zone is 60 cm deep. Select the emitter, find the set time, the emitters and flow per hectare, the irrigation interval and the seasonal volume.}\sol
\ans Soil S05 (sandy loam, sand 65, clay 10 per cent): $\theta_{33}=0.1650$, $\theta_{1500}=0.0717$, $K_s=44.7$ mm\,h$^{-1}$; the readily available fraction is 0.5, so the water to be replaced at the trigger is $0.5(\theta_{33}-\theta_{1500})=0.0466$
\ans Net depth to the trigger $Z_n=9$ mm per application of the wetted area; plant spacing: laterals every $S_l=1.5$ m, emitters every $S_e$ as tried below
\ans Trial online emitter, $q=4$ L\,h$^{-1}$ at $S_e=0.75$ m: emitter uniformity $E_u=0.890$; wetted area per emitter $A_e=S_eS_l=1.12$ m$^2$; required volume $V_{req}=Z_nA_e=10.12$ L; set time $t=3.03$ h; $E_a=80.9$, $E_s=90.9$, deep loss $19.1$, soil evaporation $0.0$ per cent
\ans Trial inline emitter, $q=2$ L\,h$^{-1}$ at $S_e=0.5$ m: emitter uniformity $E_u=0.936$; wetted area per emitter $A_e=S_eS_l=0.75$ m$^2$; required volume $V_{req}=Z_nA_e=6.75$ L; set time $t=3.74$ h; $E_a=79.3$, $E_s=84.7$, deep loss $20.7$, soil evaporation $0.0$ per cent
\ans Trial inline emitter, $q=1.6$ L\,h$^{-1}$ at $S_e=0.4$ m: emitter uniformity $E_u=0.936$; wetted area per emitter $A_e=S_eS_l=0.60$ m$^2$; required volume $V_{req}=Z_nA_e=5.40$ L; set time $t=3.74$ h; $E_a=75.9$, $E_s=81.0$, deep loss $24.1$, soil evaporation $0.0$ per cent
\ans Choice: online emitters $q=4$ L\,h$^{-1}$ at $S_e=0.75$ m, with the highest efficiency $E_a=80.9$ per cent
\ans Emitters per hectare $=10\,000/(S_eS_l)=8889$; flow of one hectare $=8889\times4/3600=9.88$ L\,s$^{-1}$
\ans Lateral of 100 m with inlet head 10 m: the head falls along the line and the emitter discharge at the end is $97.7$ per cent of that at the head (the variation sets $E_u$)
\ans Gross volume per application $=V=q\,t=4\times3.03=12.1$ L per emitter, i.e. 10.8 mm over the hectare
\ans Irrigation interval: the crop uses 5 mm\,d$^{-1}$ in peak season and the net depth is 9 mm, so irrigate every $9/5=1.8\approx2$ days, applying $12.4$ mm gross each time
\ans Volume per season of 100 days at 5 mm\,d$^{-1}$: $5\times100/E_a=618$ mm, or $6183$ m$^3$ per hectare \finans
