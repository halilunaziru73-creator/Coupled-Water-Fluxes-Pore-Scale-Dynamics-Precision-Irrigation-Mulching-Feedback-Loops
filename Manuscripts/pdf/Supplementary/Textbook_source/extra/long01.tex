\subsection{Complete characterisation of the loam}
\exbox{Retention, conductivity and storage of a loam}{Characterise the loam S06 completely: find $S_e$, $\theta$ and $K_r$ at six suctions, then the water held between field capacity and wilting, and the total and readily available water of a root zone 0.8 m deep.}\sol
\ans Van Genuchten parameters of the loam: $\alpha=0.036$ cm$^{-1}$, $n=1.56$, so $m=1-1/n=0.359$; $\theta_r=0.05$, $\theta_s=0.43$
\ans At $h=10$ cm: \[S_e=[1+(\alpha h)^n]^{-m}\ti{i}\]
Substituting the values in equation \rfi{i},
\[S_e=0.9358\]
\par\indent We know that
\[\theta=\theta_r+(\theta_s-\theta_r)S_e\ti{ii}\]
Substituting the values in equation \rfi{ii},
\[\theta=0.4056\]
$K_r=0.215$
\ans At $h=33$ cm: \[S_e=[1+(\alpha h)^n]^{-m}\ti{iii}\]
Substituting the values in equation \rfi{iii},
\[S_e=0.7406\]
\par\indent We know that
\[\theta=\theta_r+(\theta_s-\theta_r)S_e\ti{iv}\]
Substituting the values in equation \rfi{iv},
\[\theta=0.3314\]
$K_r=0.0293$
\ans At $h=100$ cm: \[S_e=[1+(\alpha h)^n]^{-m}\ti{v}\]
Substituting the values in equation \rfi{v},
\[S_e=0.4663\]
\par\indent We know that
\[\theta=\theta_r+(\theta_s-\theta_r)S_e\ti{vi}\]

\[\theta=0.2272\]
$K_r=0.00136$
\ans At $h=330$ cm: \[S_e=[1+(\alpha h)^n]^{-m}\ti{vii}\]

\[S_e=0.2482\]
\par\indent We know that
\[\theta=\theta_r+(\theta_s-\theta_r)S_e\ti{viii}\]

\[\theta=0.1443\]
$K_r=2.77\times10^{-5}$
\ans At $h=1000$ cm: \[S_e=[1+(\alpha h)^n]^{-m}\ti{ix}\]

Substituting the values in equation \rfi{xiii},
\[TAW=67\ \text{mm}\]
