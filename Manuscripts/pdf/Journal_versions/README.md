# Submission packages

One folder per article, in the order of submission. Each article is within the limits of its journal; everything else from the full manuscripts is in its Supplementary Material S1, complete and in the order of the article.

| Folder | Journal | Article | Supplementary Material |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1_Agricultural_Water_Management_Paper1` | Agricultural Water Management | Soil Hydraulic Memory and the Recoverability of Evaporation Savings and the Closure of the Water Balance (about 7,300 words with captions, 10 figures, 5 highlights, 95 references) | S1: complete text (in the folder). S2: `../Supplementary/Paper1_Supplementary_Derivations_Data_Mechanisms.pdf` |
| `2_Agricultural_Water_Management_Paper4` | Agricultural Water Management | The Reach of Suction-Triggered Irrigation and the Marginal Fate of Irrigation Water (about 5,300 words, 6 figures, 5 highlights, 45 references) | S1: complete text (in the folder). S2: `../Supplementary/Paper4_Supplementary_Derivations_Data_Mechanisms.pdf` |
| `3_SoftwareX_SWAFL` | SoftwareX | SWAFL: An open desktop application for equation-traceable irrigation scheduling and design with a graphical interface to the Daisy model (about 2,500 words with captions, 6 multi-panel figures from the software, 5 highlights, code and software metadata, 62 references) | S1: complete text of Papers 2 and 3, merged (in the folder). S2: `../Supplementary/Paper2_Supplementary_Derivations_Data_Mechanisms.pdf` and `../Supplementary/Paper3_Supplementary_Derivations_Data_Mechanisms.pdf` |

Upload to the journal: the manuscript as the main file, S1 and S2 as Supplementary Material, the highlights (typeset in the manuscript) and the graphical abstract from `../../graphical_abstracts`. The declaration of generative AI use is to be added by the author before submission. Sources and the rebuild commands are in `../../journal`.
