# About this volume {-}

Each chapter of the first part sets one of the ten soils in Properties and shows every layer, drawn from top to bottom, with the other settings at their defaults; the three irrigation designs are shown after their run with their inputs at the defaults, and the virtual farm of each design on an irrigation day. The equations of each layer are printed once, in Volume 2. The second part shows the crop water requirement for every crop of the library on the Coarse Loam.
